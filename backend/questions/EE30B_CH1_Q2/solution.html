For the output taken across the inductor,

$$\left|\frac{V_o}{V_s}\right| = \frac{\omega L}{\sqrt{R^2 + (\omega L)^2}}.$$

At the $-3\,\mathrm{dB}$ cutoff this magnitude is $1/\sqrt{2}$, so $\omega_c L = R$. Therefore,

$$f_c = \frac{\omega_c}{2\pi} = \frac{R}{2\pi L} = {{correct_answers.frequency}}\ \mathrm{Hz}.$$
