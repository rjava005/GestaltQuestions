Given the circuit with a sinusoidal source voltage $v_s(t) = V_s \cos(\omega t)$ V, we need to find the current $i(t)$ through the circuit. **Parameters Given:** - Source voltage amplitude $V_s = 10$ V - Source frequency $\omega = 1000$ rad/s - Resistance $R = 50 \, \Omega$ - Capacitance $C = 0.01$ F - Inductance $L = 0.02$ H **Step 1: Convert Time Domain to Phasor Domain** The voltage source $v_s(t)$ is represented in phasor form as: $$ V_s = 10 \angle 0^\circ \text{ V} $$ **Step 2: Calculate Impedances** - The impedance of the resistor is: $$ Z_R = 50 \, \Omega $$ - The impedance of the inductor is $Z_L = j \omega L$: $$ Z_L = j(1000) \times (0.02) \, \Omega = j20 \, \Omega $$ - The impedance of the capacitor is $Z_C = \frac{1}{j \omega C}$: $$ Z_C = \frac{1}{j(1000 \times 0.01)} \, \Omega = -j100 \, \Omega $$ **Step 3: Find the Total Impedance $Z$** $$ Z = Z_R + Z_L + Z_C $$ - $Z = 50 + j20 - j100 \, \Omega = 50 - j80 \, \Omega$ Express $Z$ in polar form: $$ Z = \sqrt{(50)^2 + (-80)^2} \angle \tan^{-1}\left(\frac{-80}{50}\right) \, \Omega $$ - Magnitude: $\sqrt{2500 + 6400} = \sqrt{8900} \approx 94.34 \, \Omega$ - Phase: $\tan^{-1}\left(\frac{-80}{50}\right) \approx -58.00^\circ$ **Step 4: Calculate the Current $I$ using Ohm's Law** $$ I = \frac{V_s}{Z} $$ $$ I = \frac{10 \angle 0^\circ}{94.34 \angle -58.00^\circ} $$ - Magnitude: $\frac{10}{94.34} \approx 0.106 \, \text{A}$ - Phase: $0^\circ + 58.00^\circ = 58.00^\circ$ **Step 5: Convert Back to Time Domain** The time domain representation of the current is: $$ i(t) = 0.106 \cos(1000t + 58.00^\circ) \, \text{A} $$
